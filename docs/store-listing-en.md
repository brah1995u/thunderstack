## Full description

Raise a legendary temple into the skies of Olympus in Thunder Stack, a precision tower-building game where every block matters.

Time each drop, control the overhang, and keep your growing structure balanced as the tower begins to lean and sway. A perfect placement strengthens your combo, while a careless move can send the entire monument crashing down in a spectacular collapse.

Journey through the Path to Olympus and conquer 60 handcrafted trials across six divine realms. Each stage introduces new goals, speeds, materials, and hazards. Build with stone, moss-covered blocks, gold, storm-charged pieces, and cracked ruins as every temple develops its own look and rhythm.

Use divine boosters when the challenge intensifies:

- Thunder Strike centers the current block with divine precision.
- Aegis Shield protects the tower from one fatal mistake.
- Slow Time gives you a calmer moment to plan the next drop.
- Crystal Magnet helps you attract valuable bonus crystals.

Test your endurance in Endless Temple, complete Heroic Feats, collect daily rewards, and climb the Local Legends ranking on your device.

Take a break from building with Crystal Rush, a fast 30-second mini-game. Guide the magnet, catch crystals and colored gems, build multipliers, avoid cracked stone, and use lightning bonuses to survive. Your first completed run each day can earn coins and crystals, while Practice mode lets you improve your best score without limits.

Thunder Stack features:

- Skill-based tower building with visible balance, leaning, swaying, and collapse physics
- 60 campaign trials across six Olympus realms
- Endless mode for high-score runs
- Multiple block materials with distinct gameplay behavior
- Four divine boosters
- 30-second Crystal Rush mini-game
- Achievements, daily rewards, and a local leaderboard
- Premium Olympus-inspired artwork, effects, music, and sound
- Fully playable offline
- No ads and no account required

Build higher, recover from dangerous mistakes, and prove that your temple deserves a place among the legends of Olympus.
